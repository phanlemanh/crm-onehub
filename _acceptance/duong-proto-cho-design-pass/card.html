Chi ap cho dung vong nay: tu vong sau kho da co proto_route nen khong con co do

so dang ky tap trung (registry.ts) lam nguon duy nhat cua slug va states, thay vi moi proto mot route tinh khong so, hoac tu quet thu muc bang quy uoc — duoc: muc luc, tra slug la, va mot cho duy nhat de cam phep do chan quen-khai-state. Mat: them proto phai sua mot file — doi lai viec do khong lam len duoc

proto o lai vinh vien thanh phong trung bay, khong xoa o S5 — duoc: mo lai ban da duyet bat cu luc nao, proto cu thanh vat lieu cho vong sau. Mat: co y nhan rui ro drift — khong dung luoi rieng, dua vao check-types va build vi proto nam trong do thi cua chung. Day cung la ly do ky thuat de bat bac real-components

co dev la bien moi truong rieng PROTO_ROUTE, khong dung NODE_ENV !== production — duoc: bat duoc tren mot ban trien khai preview de nguoi duyet mo bang dien thoai, va co mot ten that cho host_embed.dev_flag. Mat: mot bien nua phai nuoi trong .env.example, va dev cung phai dat bien moi mo duoc — nen dev_cmd mang san PROTO_ROUTE=1

them /proto vao danh sach ANONYMOUS cua apps/app/proxy.ts de ban bam duoc mo duoc khi khong co cookie phien — co y nhan downside nang nhat cua vong: proxy thoi canh /proto nen co PROTO_ROUTE thanh lop chan DUY NHAT. Doi lai AC-2 do o dung dieu kien khong-cookie (phan biet duoc co-chan voi proxy-chan) va AC-13 do rang danh sach chi dai them dung mot muc. Hai phuong an khac deu te hon: nhet proto duoi /t la sai nghia va dung [site]; them vao UNGATED la vo ich vi UNGATED nam sau lan kiem phien

khong khai design_pass.capture_cmd trong vong nay — scripts/ui-capture.mjs la ban tham chieu mot-URL-mot-file, khong khop quy uoc dat ten ma tran <state>--<breakpoint>--<theme>.png cua design-pass. De trong thi design-pass tu chup bang Browser pane, duong no xu ly san; khai mot lenh khong khop quy uoc goi te hon de trong

khong wire executors.design.static trong vong nay — bang CT1 cua feature-loop tro config:executors.design.static; kho chi co .gate va .ui_check nen con tro do dang chet. De ngoai vi no nam o lan static chu khong phai lan proto — keo vao la phong mot vong da du viec
