====================================================================================================
Authoritative sources and data model for Vietnamese legal-document validity (hieu luc), amendment chains and consolidated versions
--- SUMMARY
As of 26 Sep 2026 there is one official place that models validity as data: vbpl.vn (Co so du lieu quoc gia ve phap luat, owned by Bo Tu phap). It has a fixed status vocabulary, typed relations, a validity-history log and some article-level records. Its public interface is only an undocumented JSON gateway behind the site, with no licence text, no bulk download and no RSS. The legally authoritative TEXT is the electronic Official Gazette (Law 64/2025/QH15 Art. 9(4) gives it the value of the original), which has two RSS feeds but no validity field. Reuse is lawful in principle: legal texts are outside copyright (IP Law Art. 15(2)), free use of the database is a statutory right (Decree 52/2015 Art. 18(2), Decision 35/2026/QD-TTg Art. 12), and the IP Law amended by Law 131/2025 (effective 1 Apr 2026) adds an AI-training use clause. Commercial providers (TVPL, LuatVietnam) sit behind Cloudflare challenges and terms that forbid automated access. TVPL also hides status and effective date from non-paying visitors. Live measurement of vbpl found it unsafe as a sole truth source: wrong document-level effective dates (Land Law, AML Law), status logs that disagree with current status, relation lists that mix noise with real amendments and miss a cross-law amendment of the Land Law, and article-level records covering only 10 of the 25 articles that Law 87/2025 amended. The rules of effect also changed under a naive reader's feet. Whether implementing decrees survive their parent flipped three times (Law 80/2015: expire together; Law 64/2025 as enacted 1 Apr 2025: survive unless conflicting; Law 87/2025 from 1 Jul 2025: expire unless declared to continue). Special-mechanism resolutions override statutes with sunset dates without amending them. Consolidated texts are now mandatory by the amending text's effective date (Ordinance 01/2026, 1 Jul 2026), with a backlog deadline of 30 Sep 2026, but existing consolidated texts of the core real-estate laws are dated 2024-2025. The recommendation is a hybrid: the page owner records the legal basis (instrument, provision path, as-of date, evidence) and approves it, while a low-volume watcher on Gazette RSS plus a watch-list of vbpl records only proposes stale flags and never sets truth.
--- TENSIONS
 * vbpl advertises 'automatic validity determination', yet live records show wrong document-level effective dates, status logs that contradict current status (five partially-expired documents flipped to 'in force' in the log on 2026-05-08), a Labour Code logged as wholly expired twice, and relation lists with implausible links and missing amendments. Official label and observed accuracy disagree.
 * Statute says database content is free and officially usable (Decree 52/2015 Arts. 4, 18; Decision 35/2026 Art. 12), but vbpl.vn disallows /api/ in robots.txt, uses reCAPTCHA, and publishes no API or licence, while a community crawler openly mirrors it under CC BY. Right of use and permission to bulk-collect are separate questions.
 * The commercial vendors add the most useful layer (clause-level change guidance, comparison, consolidated-over-time views, status) but their terms forbid automated access and building a competing database; the underlying texts are not copyrightable (IP Law Art. 15(2)), so the exposure is contract and possibly unfair-competition rather than copyright.
 * Three regimes for surviving implementing decrees (Law 80/2015 expire together; Law 64/2025 as enacted survive; Law 87/2025 expire unless declared) coexist in the same portal text, and the portal shows the enacted wording of Law 64/2025. A summariser reading the stored text gets the wrong rule.
 * Consolidated texts are legally official but lag: Land, Housing and KDBDS VBHN date from 2024-2025, and the 2026 Ordinance forces a backlog to be cleared by 30 Sep 2026. Also no consolidation is made for pilot/special-mechanism texts, exactly where real-estate and credit rules are moving.
 * Document-level status (what hobby RAG projects filter on) hides article-level reality: 5,235 documents are 'partly expired' with no article list, and the portal recorded only 10 of 25 amended articles for the drafting law. Filtering out fully-expired documents is safe only if the status is right, which the Labour Code episode shows it was not.
 * Gazette (original-value text) and the database (official-use text, plus status) are both official but neither is real time: the Gazette carried decrees signed 15-17 Sep in an issue dated 25 Sep, and the database is allowed 15 working days. RSS pubDate is the signing date, not the posting time.
 * The community dataset justifies 'public domain' by citing Law 104/2016, which Law 01/2026 replaced on 1 Sep 2026; the sounder basis is IP Law Art. 15(2). Licence rationale drifts with the law.

 > (b) SCHEMA 3/3 - mapping to generic page fields and internal documents.  > (c) RULES for marking pages stale (evaluate on every change signal; each cited (instrument, provision) pair is the unit): R1 HARD-STALE when a relation of type sua doi | thay the | bai bo | dinh chinh | dinh chi | tam ngung | cong bo het hieu luc targets a cited provision path. R2 HARD-STALE when the cited instrument status becomes HHL or TNHL, or its parent is replaced after 1 Jul 2025 and no 'cong bo tiep tuc con hieu luc' document exists (children expire by default). R3 SOFT-STALE (owner check, page stays visible with banner) when status becomes HHL1P or relation scope is toan bo / path unknown, or when the content_hash of a cited instrument changes with no relation (correction; the database must fix discrepancies within 1 working day). R4 TIME-TRIGGERED: on each cited provision's or pending amendment's future effective_from or effective_to, set stale_after to that date, show a 'watch' banner 30 days ahead, flip on the day (e.g. Law 23/2026 on 1 Dec 2026, Resolution 66.23/2026 sunset 28 Feb 2027, district-level instruments 28 Feb 2027). R5 CLAUSE DETECTOR on every newly ingested instrument: find amending clauses that name a cited instrument (Sua doi, bo sung | Bai bo | Thay the ... khoan/Dieu/diem ... cua <loai> so <so hieu>) and priority clauses ('neu khac nhau thi thuc hien theo ...'), because the portal missed the Public Investment Law amendment of the Land Law and does not model overrides. R6 NEVER TRUST A FEED FLIP ALONE: a whole-document HHL, a scheduler-logged status change, or a relation from a lower-ranking or local instrument to a national one needs a second signal (Gazette item or amending text naming the provision) before HARD-STALE; log the conflict as a data conflict, not a legal change (the Labour Code was logged as wholly expired twice in 2026). R7 CONSOLIDATED TEXT: treat VBHN as a dated snapshot (as_of = signing date); if amendments effective after that date exist, apply them explicitly; on any mismatch the source instrument prevails. R8 INTERNAL DOCS: when a can_cu_phap_ly target changes, flag the internal document; also flag on review_due (AML annual review) and when registration status changes. R9 EVENT-DATE: pages that cite transitional articles must state which event dates the rule covers; the agent asks for or infers the event date before answering.
 > (c) CONFLICT CARD shown to the page owner on R1-R3 hits (batch by instrument, not by page): (1) severity: Critical (cited rule repealed, replaced, suspended or child instrument lapsed), Major (cited provision amended), Minor (correction, hash change), Info (future event); (2) what changed: source instrument (so ky hieu, type, agency, signed and effective dates, Gazette link), relation type, target provision paths, before/after text (amending text, or VBHN where available) with a diff; (3) timeline: effective_from/to, transitional clause excerpt and the event-date window; (4) evidence and agreement: which sources say it (Gazette, vbpl relation, vbpl history, VBHN, clause detector) and where they disagree, shown as data conflicts; (5) hierarchy check per Law 64/2025 Art. 58 (higher rank prevails, later same-body prevails, override clause, treaty, local territorial scope); (6) affected paragraphs and any internal documents that cite the page; (7) actions with one recommended default: 'Apply AI-drafted update as diff' | 'No impact (reason required)' | 'Schedule until effective date' | 'Retire page' | 'Ask legal counsel'; (8) audit line: who, when, evidence hash, new valid_as_of. Show only the recommended action prominently to keep owner effort at one tap.
 > (d) RECOMMENDATION: hybrid, with manual entry as the authority and an external feed as a signal. Owner-approved legal-basis records (instrument, provision path, as_of, evidence link, hash) are the source of truth; a watcher proposes changes and never approves. Watcher: (i) Gazette RSS both feeds every 20-60 minutes for new instruments, corrections, VBHN and declarations; (ii) daily sitemap-shard-1 lastmod diff on vbpl for new records; (iii) daily per-instrument fetch of detail, /history and /history-reference for the watch-list only (about 60-150 instruments plus their parents and children, roughly 450 requests per day), at one request per second or slower, identifying user agent with contact address, caching, no bulk mirror; (iv) clause detector on new texts. Reasons: portal data errors above; no SLA, licence or API documentation; statutory posting lag up to 3-5 weeks; consolidated texts lag. LEGAL AND SCRAPING RISK: IP risk is low for texts (IP Law Art. 15(2), Decree 52/2015 Art. 18(2), Decision 35/2026 Art. 12, IP Law Art. 7(5) from 1 Apr 2026); the real risks are technical and contractual: the gateway is undocumented and may close; vbpl robots.txt disallows /api/ (the gateway host has no robots.txt); some flows use reCAPTCHA and endpoints return 401 (never bypass); Gazette RSS terms cover only individuals and non-profits and are revocable, so ask the Government Office/MoJ in writing for permission or an arrangement and cite congbao.chinhphu.vn; do NOT ingest TVPL or LuatVietnam programmatically (terms forbid automated access and building a competing database, and Cloudflare blocks scripts): a single TVPL Basic seat (147,000 VND/month) is a fair human cross-check for owners on disputed items. Get counsel to confirm the Vietnamese unauthorised-access and unfair-competition angles for the scraping path (inference; not legal advice).
 > AGENT ANSWERING POLICY: answer only from approved pages; every claim carries citation (instrument, provision path, text_version_hash, valid_as_of); pages in stale_hard are excluded from answers and the agent says the topic is pending review; stale_soft pages are answered with a visible caveat naming the pending change; pages with 'watch' show the upcoming date; questions with an event date before valid_as_of are routed to transitional provisions or refused; add a standing disclaimer that answers are reference material (the State portal takes the same position for AI output).
 > EVALUATION SET plan (5): build time-sliced questions from the vbpl relation graph and the CC BY community snapshot (cross-checked against Gazette text, not against the snapshot alone): post-amendment; pre-amendment (event date); child decree after parent replaced (three regimes); cross-law hidden amendment (Land Law Art. 67(4)(a) via Public Investment Law); override with sunset (Resolution 254/2025, 66.23/2026); future effective date (Law 23/2026 on 1 Dec 2026); wrong portal effective date (Land Law, AML); internal regulation registration (+15 days). Do not use the 2021 Zalo corpus for current-validity truth; use ViHERMES and the German temporal benchmark only for method ideas (licences unclear).
 > BUILD ORDER: first the citation table and provision_state for the roughly 40 real-estate and credit instruments named above (Land, Housing, KDBDS, Credit Institutions, AML, drafting laws and their decrees), then the Gazette watcher plus R1-R4, then the clause detector (R5), then the conflict card.
